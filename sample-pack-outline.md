# Sample ClosePack outline (MVP deliverable)

1. Cover — firm logo, client name, period (e.g. August 2026)
2. Snapshot — Revenue, Expenses, Net profit, Cash (if provided), MoM deltas
3. What changed — 3–5 bullet narrative (AI draft, human-editable)
4. Income detail — top revenue lines / notable swings
5. Expense detail — top categories / notable swings
6. Questions for the client call — optional checklist
7. Disclaimer — prepared from client books; not audited advice
