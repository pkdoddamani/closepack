Who this is for

  • Independent bookkeepers and tiny firms with ~10–40 monthly clients
  • People who outgrew copy-paste Excel packs but do not need a full forecasting suite
  • Anyone paying per-company reporting tools that get expensive as the client list grows

Fathom Starter is about A$59 per company / month at the entry tier. ClosePack is a flat $79/mo for up to 25 packs — built for the recurring narrative job, not deep BI.
