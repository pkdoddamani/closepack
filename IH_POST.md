# Indie Hackers draft

Title: Validating a flat-fee monthly client pack tool for indie bookkeepers — what am I missing?

Body:

Building in public / looking for signal.

**Problem hypothesis:** Independent bookkeepers with ~10–40 clients still spend meaningful time each month turning clean books into a *client-ready* pack (branded PDF / narrative). Tools like Fathom are strong but price per company, which hurts solos with many small clients.

**Wedge:** Upload QBO/Xero export → branded PDF with KPIs + editable plain-English commentary. Flat **$79/mo** for up to 25 packs. Not a Fathom clone — no forecasting suite in v1.

**Live waitlist:** https://pkdoddamani.github.io/closepack/

Questions for anyone who has sold to bookkeepers / accountants, or *is* one:

1. Is "client pack assembly" a real monthly time sink, or do most just email the default QBO PDF?
2. Would flat pricing matter vs per-entity BI tools?
3. What would make you distrust an AI narrative on financials?

Happy to take brutal "this is dead" feedback.
